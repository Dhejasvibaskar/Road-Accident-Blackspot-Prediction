Data and Methodology

This project uses UK Department for Transport road collision records from 2021 to 2025. Historical blackspots are identified by grouping nearby collision coordinates and calculating severity-weighted scores.

For route visualization, the application identifies recorded blackspots located within approximately 100 metres of the calculated driving route.

Risk categories are project-defined classifications of historical collision concentration and severity. They are not official road safety ratings.

About This Project

Road Accident Blackspot Prediction and Route-Based Visualization Using Interactive Maps is a Principles of Data Science project investigating historical road collision patterns and their relationship to selected driving routes.

This tool is for educational and research purposes. Historical collision patterns do not guarantee future accident locations. Always follow official road signs, traffic regulations and current road conditions.
